# Warforged

Warforged are sentient, humanoid construct-lineages forged from wood, stone, and metal, infused with life through arcane alchemy inside creation forges. Originally mass-produced by [[house-cannith]] as mindless soldier units for [[last-war|The Last War]], an unexpected breakthrough in 965 YK granted them true self-awareness, emotion, and free will.

Following the Treaty of Thronehold in 996 YK, Warforged were legally recognized as free people rather than property, and creation forges were outlawed.

## Physical Characteristics

* **Composition:** Rooted in a core skeleton of wood-fiber bundles, plated with steel, darkwood, or stone, and energized by alchemical fluid circulating through conduits.
* **Living Constructs:** While immune to disease and non-breathing, Warforged can be healed through both repair magic and conventional healing spells.
* **Plating & Modifications:** Many Warforged modify their physical frames with specialized tools, armor plates, or weapons built directly into their limbs.

## Societal Standing

Struggling to find identity and purpose after a century of warfare, many Warforged work as manual laborers in [[sharn]], mercenaries for dragonmarked houses, or disciples of the radical warlord known as the **Lord of Blades**.
