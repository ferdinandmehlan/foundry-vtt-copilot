# Kalashtar

The Kalashtar are a compound race formed by the merging of human hosts with renegade light-spirits (*quori*) from **Dal Quor**, the Region of Dreams. Fleeing the nightmare-entities that dominate their home plane, these benevolent spirits bound themselves to human bloodlines centuries ago, granting their descendants inherent telepathic abilities and heightened spiritual awareness.

To common folk across Khorvaire, Kalashtar look like exceptionally graceful, serene humans, but their eyes possess a subtle luminescent sheen and their presence exudes a calm authority.

## Traits & Telepathy

* **Dual Soul:** Every Kalashtar shares a spiritual connection with a quori spirit, influencing their personality, dreamless sleep, and emotional tendencies.
* **Mind-Link:** Capable of effortless non-verbal telepathic communication over short distances.
* **Psionic Affinity:** Natural inclination toward martial discipline, monk traditions, and psionic abilities.

## The Secret War

The Kalashtar are locked in a silent, psychic war against the **Dreaming Dark**—an organization of nightmare-spirits operating through mind-controlled human puppets and corrupt officials in commercial centers like [[sharn]].
