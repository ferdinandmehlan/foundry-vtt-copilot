# The Sovereign Host

The Sovereign Host is the dominant pantheon across Khorvaire. Comprising nine benevolent deities representing civilization, agriculture, honor, and nature, the Host is not governed by a single centralized church, but by loosely connected shrines, temples, and community priests.

Most citizens of Galifar offer prayers to specific Sovereigns depending on their immediate need—a blacksmith prays to Onatar, while a sailor prays to Kol Korran.

## The Nine Sovereigns

* **Arawai:** Sovereign of Agriculture and Abundance.
* **Aureon:** Sovereign of Law and Knowledge.
* **Boldrei:** Sovereign of Community and Home.
* **Dol Arrah:** Sovereign of Honor and Sacrifice.
* **Dol Dorn:** Sovereign of Strength and Arms.
* **Kol Korran:** Sovereign of World and Wealth.
* **Olladra:** Sovereign of Feast and Fortune.
* **Onatar:** Sovereign of Forge and Fire.
* **Vassalia:** Sovereign of High and Universal Knowledge.

## Cultural Role

Unlike the [[church-of-the-silver-flame|Silver Flame]], the Sovereign Host rarely conducts holy wars or crusades. Its clerics act as civic advisors, judges, and community healers in places like [[sharn]] and Wroat.
