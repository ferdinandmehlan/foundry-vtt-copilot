# The Church of the Silver Flame

The Church of the Silver Flame is a zealous lawful-good religion dedicated to protecting the innocent from supernatural evil, fiends, and undead. Founded when the paladin Tira Miron sacrificed herself to bind a powerful demon overlord, her spirit merged with the pillar of argent fire in **Flamekeep**.

Unlike many traditional pantheons, the Silver Flame does not demand worship of distant deities, but rather participation in an active spiritual force for good.

## Key Principles & Doctrine

* **Protect the Innocent:** Defend common folk against monsters, extraplanar threats, and dark magic.
* **Purge Evil:** Seek out fiendish corruption, lycanthropy, and necromancy wherever it takes root.
* **The Pure Flame:** A hardline fanatic faction within the Church that preaches intolerance and violent purges against non-human species and aberrant mark wielders.

## Local Presence & Operations

In major cities like [[sharn]], the Church operates cathedral enclaves, soup kitchens, and garrisons of templars. While popular among the working class in [[dura|Dura]], the local hierarchy frequently clashes with criminal organizations like the [[sharn-boromar-clan|Boromar Clan]].
