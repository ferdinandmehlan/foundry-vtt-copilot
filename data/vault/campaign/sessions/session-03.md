## GM Prep & Situation

### Strong Start
* Rain pours over Lower Dura. A bloodied messenger from the [[sharn-boromar-clan]] stumbles into the PCs' safehouse, dropping a cracked conduit lens before collapsing.

### Faction Directives Tonight
* **Boromar Clan:** Wants the stolen dragonshard crate back at any cost to maintain market authority.

## Session Recap

The party met with [[lady-hazel]] at the [[sharn-gold-dragon-inn]] to negotiate retrieval of the missing shipment. After tracking the couriers down into the lower levels of [[sharn-dura]], they were ambushed on a rain-slicked skybridge by Tarkanan mercenaries.

After a tight combat, the PCs secured the crate intact and decided to inspect its contents before returning it to the Boromar safehouse.
