## Session Recap

The party met [[lady-hazel]] at her Bazaar stall and persuaded her to share what she knew about the missing shipment. She pointed them toward the Boromar warehouse in Lower [[sharn-dura]], though her hesitation suggested she was holding something back.

At the warehouse, the party found the loading manifest had been altered and spotted a [[house-cannith]] agent slipping out a side door. Their questioning of the foreman confirmed the crate was moved at night under a forged header. The trail now points to the lower sewer skybridges — and to whoever is moving the crate under cover of the coming storm.
