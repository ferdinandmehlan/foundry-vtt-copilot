## Session Recap

The party was approached by a Boromar agent at the [[sharn-gold-dragon-inn]] who presented a debt ledger listing each PC as owing the clan. Rather than start a confrontation with the heavily armed enforcers watching the exits, the party agreed to hear [[saidan-boromar]] out.

The patriarch offered a simple proposition: clear the debts in exchange for a single job — retrieving a cargo shipment that went missing from a Boromar warehouse in Lower [[sharn-dura]]. The party left the inn with the first lead: the last person to log the shipment's movement was a street medic named [[lady-hazel]].
