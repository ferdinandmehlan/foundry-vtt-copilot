# Encounter: Dura Sewer Run

Tarkanan couriers abandon the skybridges and flee into the storm sewers with the crate, forcing the party into a running chase through flooded tunnels as the rain surge rises.

## Tactical Environment
* **Rising Water:** At the start of round 3, water depth rises to chest height, halving movement and granting disadvantage on melee attack rolls.
* **Sewer Current:** Any creature knocked prone is carried 10ft downstream toward the drain grate.
* **Narrow Ledges:** A crumbling walkway lines one wall — only Medium or smaller creatures may use it, and it requires a **DC 10 Acrobatics** check to cross at speed.

## Adversaries
* **2x Tarkanan Couriers** (Fast scouts with daggers and smoke bombs)
* **2x Sewer Crocodiles** (Lurking in the deep water; they attack whoever splashes last)

## Hazards & Features
* **Drain Grate:** A rusted grate at the tunnel's end blocks the exit. **DC 14 Strength** to wrench open, or a pick/lock at **DC 15 Thieves' Tools**.
* **Flood Debris:** Crates and refuse bob in the current — usable as improvised cover (half cover).

## Rewards / Loot
* 20 gp in soaked coin pouches.
* The crate's shipping manifest (a forged Cannith header, per Session 02).
* One smoke bomb and one flash pellet.

## GM Notes
* The couriers are decoys. The real crate is already moving through the upper tunnels; if the party captures a courier alive, they learn the route but the courier lies about the destination (Callestan instead of the true safehouse).
* If the party is losing badly, the crocodiles turn on the wounded couriers instead, buying the party an opening.
