# Encounter: Dura Skybridge Ambush

## Tactical Environment
* **Heavy Rain:** Light obscurement; disadvantage on long-range Perception checks.
* **Slippery Ledge:** Any creature taking forced movement near the edge must succeed on a **DC 12 Reflex/Acrobatics save** or grab the railing as a reaction.

## Adversaries
* **1x Tarkanan Lieutenant** (Uses Assassin / Spy stat block)
* **3x Tarkanan Thugs** (Brutes with shortbows and daggers)

## Hazards & Features
* **Lightning Conduit Railing:** Touching the conduit housing while dealing electric/lightning damage deals `2d6` lightning damage to adjacent targets.

## Rewards / Loot
* 35 gp in mixed coin.
* 1x *Potion of Healing*.
* Encrypted Tarkanan dispatch note referencing [[lady-hazel]].
