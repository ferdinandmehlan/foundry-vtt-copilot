# Quest: The Boromar Debt

[[saidan-boromar]] has offered to clear the party's outstanding debt if they retrieve a stolen cargo shipment intercepted by the [[tarkanan-syndicate]] in Lower Dura.

## Objectives
- [x] Meet [[lady-hazel]] at the [[sharn-gold-dragon-inn]] for local intel.
- [x] Intercept the Tarkanan courier on the [[sharn-dura]] skybridge.
- [ ] Deliver the sealed crate to the Boromar safehouse in Callestan.

<section class="secret" style="background: rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08); border-left: 4px solid #dc2626; padding: 16px;">

## Secret Twists & Mechanics
* **Hidden Objective:** If the PCs inspect the crate's internal lock (DC 16 Arcana), they discover a prototype [[house-cannith]] creation schema.
* **Branching Outcome:**
  * **Deliver unopened:** Boromar clears the debt + gives 200 gp.
  * **Deliver opened / Tampered:** Saidan honors the debt clearance but quietly marks the party as a liability.
  * **Sell to Cannith:** Earn 500 gp, but gain the **Hostile** status with [[sharn-boromar-clan]].
