# Quest: Hunt for the Schema

When the party cracks the crate's internal lock (DC 16 Arcana), they find it holds no dragonshards — only a prototype warforged creation schema stamped with an unfinished [[house-cannith]] seal. [[lady-hazel]] recognizes the mark and warns the party that either Cannith or the [[tarkanan-syndicate]] will kill to control it.

## Objectives
- [ ] Identify who authorized the schema's production (paper trail at the Dura foundry).
- [ ] Locate the [[house-cannith]] agent who fled the warehouse in Session 02.
- [ ] Decide the schema's fate: return it to Cannith, destroy it, or keep it.

<section class="secret" style="background: rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08); border-left: 4px solid #dc2626; padding: 16px;">

## Secret Twists & Mechanics
* **True Owner:** The schema was commissioned by a rogue Cannith splinter cell for an experimental warforged frame — one with a personality matrix the house formally banned after the Last War.
* **Timeline Pressure:** Every day the party holds the schema, Tarkanan and Cannith both escalate their pursuit (street tags appear, safehouse is watched, an ambush is set at the sewer run).
* **Branching Outcome:**
  * **Return to Cannith:** 500 gp + a Cannith favor, but the splinter cell's identity stays hidden.
  * **Destroy it:** Earn [[lady-hazel]]'s lasting trust and avoid house attention, but forgo the reward.
  * **Keep it / Sell to Tarkanan:** 800 gp, but gain **Hostile** status with [[house-cannith]] and a personal hunt from the splinter cell.
