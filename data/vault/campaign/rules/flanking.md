# House Rule: Tactical Flanking

To encourage dynamic positioning without trivializing abilities that grant Advantage (such as Reckless Attack or Spells), this campaign uses **Flat Numerical Flanking**.

## Rules Mechanics

* **Flanking Requirement:** When an attacker and at least one ally are on opposite sides of a target within melee range.
* **Bonus:** Melee attack rolls against the flanked target gain a **+2 bonus to hit**.
* **Cover Exception:** A target whose back is positioned directly against a solid stone wall, pillar, or enclosed structure cannot be flanked from behind.
