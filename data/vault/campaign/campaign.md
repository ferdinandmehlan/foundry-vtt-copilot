# City of Towers (A Spooktober Special)

> *"In the vertical labyrinth of Sharn, the real monsters rarely live in the shadows—they buy real estate in the Upper Wards while paying street gangs to bleed the lower levels."*

---

## Campaign Premise
A high-stakes, noir-themed autumn campaign set in **Sharn, City of Towers**. The party operates as freelance trouble-shooters navigating a escalating shadow war between the **[[sharn-boromar-clan]]** and the **[[tarkanan-syndicate]]**.

As autumn weather rolls over the city, strange eldritch surges and grim murders are breaking out across the lower levels, pointing to a eerie threat lurking beneath the city's foundations.

## GM Secrets: Macro Arc & Endgame

### The True Antagonist
The syndicate clashes are a deliberate distraction. **[[house-cannith]]** rogue artificer *Vaelen* is conducting necromantic schema tests beneath the city to build an undead-powered warforged chassis (The Spooktober Feature).

### Upcoming High-Level Plot Beats
1. **The Blood Moon Festival (Target: Session 05):**
    - A city-wide gala in Skyway. The party must infiltrate the event to stop an assassination attempt on [[saidan-boromar]].
2. **The Sewer Breach (Target: Session 07):**
    - Vaelen activates the subterranean schema node, causing mass undead surges through the Lower Dura drainage systems.
3. **Climax — The Lightning Rail Heist:**
    - The final confrontation takes place aboard an out-of-control lightning rail carrying the completed prototype schema out of Sharn.
