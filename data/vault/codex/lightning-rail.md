# The Lightning Rail

The Lightning Rail is Khorvaire's premier overland transit network. Managed and operated exclusively by **House Orien**, the rail connects major cities across Breland, Aundair, Thrane, and Zilargo using levitating coach trains powered by bound air elementals.

Rather than running on traditional physical tracks, the coaches hover roughly a foot above line paths constructed of conductor stones set into the ground.

## Technical Specifications

* **Propulsion:** Driven by a bound air or fire elemental contained within a specially attuned [[concept-dragonshards|Khyber dragonshard]] housing inside the lead engine car.
* **Conductor Stones:** A continuous line of magical stones laid across the continent. When the engine passes over them, electrical arcs form between the train and the stones, creating a crackling blue lightning field that propels and levitates the train.
* **Top Speed:** Approximately 30 miles per hour (continuous travel day and night).

## Major Hubs & Infrastructure

* **Central Terminus:** Major stations exist in core industrial hubs like [[sharn]], Wroat, and Passage.
* **Post-War Disruptions:** The shattering of the nation of Cyre during the Last War severed key transcontinental conductor stone lines, leaving eastern Khorvaire isolated from the western rail network.
