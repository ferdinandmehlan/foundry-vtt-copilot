# The Day of Mourning

On 20 Olarune 994 YK, an unprecedented magical cataclysm swept across the central nation of Cyre. A wall of gray, dead mist rolled outward from the heartland, killing or mutating every living creature in its path and transforming the fertile realm into a desolate, magically warped wasteland now known as the **Mournland**.

The true cause of the Mourning remains one of the greatest unsolved mysteries in Eberron. Speculation ranges from a failed [[house-cannith]] superweapon to divine punishment or planar convergence.

## Lasting Consequences

* **End of the Last War:** Terrified that the same fate might strike their own capitals, the warring leaders agreed to a ceasefire, leading directly to the [[last-war|Last War]] ending.
* **Refugee Crisis:** Hundreds of thousands of Cyran survivors were left homeless, relocating to slums like [[dura|Dura in Sharn]] or frontier settlements in [[breland]].
* **The Dead Mist:** The perimeter of mist surrounding the Mournland still persists today, stopping spell effects, deadening natural healing, and preserving corpse-filled battlefields in terrifying perfection.
