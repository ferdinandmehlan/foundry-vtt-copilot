# Dragonshards

Dragonshards are translucent, room-temperature crystals imbued with raw elemental and magical energy. Found across Eberron, they serve as the foundational power source for almost all advanced magical technology, elemental binding, and dragonmark-attuned focus items.

There are three distinct varieties, each tied to one of the mythical Progenitor Dragons:

## Types of Dragonshards

### Siberys Dragonshards (Sky)
* **Appearance:** Translucent amber with swirling golden veins, found in equatorial meteor showers.
* **Primary Use:** Required to forge dragonmark focus items and amplify inherent dragonmarked heir abilities. Highly sought after by Dragonmarked Houses like [[house-cannith]].

### Eberron Dragonshards (Earth)
* **Appearance:** Crimson-veined crystals embedded deep within soil and rock layers.
* **Primary Use:** The universal battery of magical engineering. Used in storing spell effects, powering elemental engines, and crafting warforged cores.

### Khyber Dragonshards (Underdark)
* **Appearance:** Deep night-blue or violet crystals found near fiendish manifest zones and underground caverns.
* **Primary Use:** Used to bind elementals, fiends, and extraplanar entities. Essential for powering high-speed transit like the [[concept-lightning-rail|Lightning Rail]].

## Market & Industry

Because refining dragonshards requires specialized tools, house monopolies control the refining process. Raw shards are harvested in hazardous zones like Q'barra or Xen'drik, shipped via local hubs like [[sharn]], and processed in main research enclaves.
