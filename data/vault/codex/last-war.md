# The Last War

The Last War was a century-long global civil war that shattered the United Kingdom of Galifar. Following the death of King Jarot in 894 YK, his five children refused to honor the traditional order of succession, causing the five crown provinces—Breland, Aundair, Karrnath, Thrane, and Cyre—to splinter into independent warring nations.

The war transformed Khorvaire's political landscape, spurred unprecedented breakthroughs in arcane technology (such as the [[warforged]] and [[dragonshards|dragonshard weaponry]]), and devastated the continent's infrastructure.

## Key Eras & Milestones

1. **894 YK — Succession Crisis:** War erupts across all borders.
2. **965 YK — Creation of Warforged:** [[house-cannith]] deploys sentient soldiers to the frontlines.
3. **994 YK — The Day of Mourning:** A mysterious cataclysm completely destroys Cyre in a single day, abruptly halting global aggression out of mutual fear.
4. **996 YK — Treaty of Thronehold:** Formal peace agreement signed, officially ending the war and dissolving Galifar.
