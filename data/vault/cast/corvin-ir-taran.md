# Corvin ir'Taran

A former combat engineer for [[house-cannith]] during [[lore-last-war|The Last War]], Corvin survived the cataclysmic destruction of Cyre on the day of the Mourning. Scarred by the memories of the frontline, he broke ties with the House's official enclaves and disappeared into the lower levels of [[sharn]].

Today, Corvin operates out of a cramped, lightning-singed workshop in [[dura|Dura]], offering off-the-books repairs for magical conduits, elemental engines, and warforged plating.

## Personality & Appearance
Corvin is lean, perpetual-motion jittery, and smells constantly of ozone and alchemical solvent. He wears a custom-fitted suit of thunder-forged plate covered in micro-runes, usually hidden under a patched leather duster.

* **Flaw:** Deeply paranoid about [[house-cannith]] discovery agents finding his personal workshop and reclamation schematics.
* **Goal:** Uncover the true origin of the Mourning and reverse-engineer forbidden Schema blueprints without attracting the attention of Baron Merrix d'Cannith.

## Relationships & Hooks
* **[[lady-hazel]]:** Hired her as a field assistant and courier after she helped him slip past a [[sharn-watch]] checkpoint.
* **[[saidan-boromar]]:** Owes a significant debt to the [[sharn-boromar-clan|Boromar Clan]] for illegal access to refined [[concept-dragonshards|Eberron dragonshards]].
