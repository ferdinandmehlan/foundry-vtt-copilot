# Saidan Boromar

Saidan Boromar is the soft-spoken, immaculate patriarch of the [[sharn-boromar-clan|Boromar Clan]]. To the public and members of the City Council, he presents himself as a wealthy, retired merchant and generous civic benefactor. In reality, he sits at the apex of the largest criminal empire in [[sharn]], directing protection rackets, smuggling routes, and bribery networks from his lavish estate.

Sensing his age and facing rising pressure from the violent [[sharn-tarkanan-syndicate|Tarkanan Syndicate]], Saidan has become increasingly ruthless in protecting his family's hegemony over the city's underworld.

## Personality & Interactions
Saidan never raises his voice and rarely issues direct threats. Instead, he offers lavish hospitality, fine wine, and "favors"—making it clear that accepting his generosity binds you to his service.

* **Tactics:** Prefers leverage over bloodshed. If forced into violence, he deploys highly trained halfling clawfoot-riders and stealthy assassins rather than street thugs.
* **Current Focus:** Securing exclusive distribution rights for dreamlily shipments coming out of Q'barra through [[dura|Dura's lower docks]].

## Connections
* **[[corvin-ir-taran]]:** Holds Corvin's unpaid material debt and occasionally calls upon him to repair sensitive, black-market magical artifacts.
* **[[sharn-boromar-clan]]:** Commands absolute loyalty from the clan's inner circle through a combination of familial devotion and swift retribution.
