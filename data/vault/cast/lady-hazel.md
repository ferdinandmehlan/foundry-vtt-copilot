# Lady Hazel

Despite her noble-sounding moniker, "Lady" Hazel is a quick-witted street medic and pickpocket who grew up in the crowded alleyways of Lower Dura. She possesses an unregistered, faint Mark of Healing, which she uses to patch up mercenaries, smugglers, and desperate street urchins without charging House fees.

Hazel attached herself to the party as a field medic, guide, and scout after pulling [[corvin-ir-taran]] out of a collapsed conduit shaft in [[dura|Dura]].

## Mannerisms & Role
Hazel speaks in rapid-fire Sharn street slang and is rarely seen without a bandolier of tourniquets, scalpels, and stolen alchemical salves.

* **Combat Role:** Secondary healer and scout. She avoids direct frontline clashes, preferring to sling blinding flash-powder or stabilize downed party members under cover of darkness.
* **Quirk:** Refuses to enter high-end establishments in Upper Sharn unless she's actively disguised or armed with a stolen invitation.

## GM Secrets
<section class="secret" style="background: rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08); border-left: 4px solid #dc2626; padding: 16px;">
Hazel is quietly passing low-level information to a mid-tier lieutenant in the [[sharn-boromar-clan|Boromar Clan]]. She isn't malicious—the Clan controls her younger brother's debt, and she provides intelligence on local shipments to keep her family safe.
</section>
