# Cogsbridge Watch Post

A Sharn Watch station embedded in Dura's lower levels, serving as the primary customs and law enforcement checkpoint for the Cogsbridge—a critical thoroughfare connecting the Cogs industrial district to the rest of the lower city. The station's iron-and-stone facade blends into the cramped corridor, its lanterns casting a perpetual amber glow over the bustling checkpoint.

## Details & Overview

* **Location:** Level 3 of Dura, situated directly adjacent to the Cogsbridge archway.
* **Primary Function:** Customs enforcement, contraband interdiction, and general law enforcement along the bridge and surrounding corridors.
* **Demeanor:** Officious but pragmatic. The watch here deals with a steady stream of workers, merchants, and cargo crossing from the Cogs, resulting in a gruff professionalism tempered by the sheer volume of traffic.
* **Atmosphere:** The constant clang of the Cogs echoes through the stone, mingling with the murmured complaints of travelers awaiting inspection.

## Notable Features & Points of Interest

* **The Iron Gate:** The station's namesake checkpoint—a pair of reinforced iron grilles that can seal the Cogsbridge passage in emergencies. During normal operations, only one lane remains open while the other is used for secondary inspections.
* **Holding Cells:** Three cramped cells carved from solid stone, typically used to hold contraband runners and those unable to pay fines on the spot.
* **Watch Commander's Office:** A modestly appointed office on the upper level, where Inspector Threnn Hask manages the station's operations and reviews daily reports.

## People & Organizations

* **[[sharn-watch|Sharn Watch]]:** The municipal law enforcement body responsible for maintaining order across all levels of Sharn. The Cogsbridge station fields twelve officers across three shifts.
* **Inspector Threnn Hask:** The station's sergeant, a grizzled half-elf with a keen eye for smuggling patterns. Known for his rigid adherence to protocol—and his quiet suspicions about corruption within his own ranks.
* **[[the-cogsmen|The Cogsmen]]:** A street gang with strong ties to the Cogs district. They control much of the unofficial labor at the bridge and have a complicated relationship with the watch—sometimes cooperative, often antagonistic.

### GM Secrets

* **The Hidden Passage:** Behind the armory storage room, a loose stone slab conceals a narrow passage leading into the lower sewers. Smugglers have used this route for years to move contraband around the checkpoint. A DC 14 Investigation check reveals the subtle signs of recent passage—scuff marks, disturbed dust, faint odor of canal water.
* **Corrupt Officer Davin:** Officer Davin, a junior inspector, is on the payroll of the [[daash-racketeers|daash racketeers]]. He provides advance warning of inspections and occasionally "misplaces" evidence. He appears helpful and friendly—the sort of officer who always volunteers for the night shift.
