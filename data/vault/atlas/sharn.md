# Sharn, City of Towers

Rising thousands of feet into the sky above the Dagger River, Sharn is the largest metropolis in Khorvaire. Thanks to a powerful localized manifest zone tied to the celestial plane of Syrania, levitation and flight magic are heavily amplified, allowing architects to build soaring spires that defy gravity.

The city is structured both vertically (Upper, Middle, and Lower Wards) and horizontally (Plateaus/Quarters). Wealth, prestige, and fresh air increase the higher up you go, while light and safety diminish near the base.

## Major Quarters & Districts

* **Central Plateau:** The administrative and financial heart of Sharn.
* **[[dura|Dura]]:** The oldest, poorest, and most sprawling quarter of the city.
* **Menthis Plateau:** The cultural hub, housing theater districts and Morgrave University.
* **The Cogs:** The dark, fiery industrial base located far beneath the city streets where [[warforged]] labor at giant foundries.

## Key Lore

* **Vertical Living:** Skybridges, magic-powered lifts, and soaring towers define daily movement.
* **Post-War Crucible:** Spies, veterans from [[last-war|The Last War]], dragonmarked houses, and criminal syndicates mix in a delicate cold war atmosphere.
