# Dura

Dura is the oldest and largest quarter in [[sharn]]. Originally built over the ruins of ancient goblin cities, the rest of Sharn has largely outgrown and neglected Dura. Today, it is a bustling, crowded, and impoverished sector defined by crumbling stone, burned-out everbright lanterns, and narrow, shadowed skybridges.

While considered dirty and dangerous by the elites of Upper Sharn, Dura is a thriving hub of opportunity, illegal trade, and working-class grit.

## Key Wards & Neighborhoods

* **Upper Dura:** Features the [[dura-clifftop|Clifftop]] adventurer's quarter and garrisons. Prosperous compared to the lower levels.
* **Middle Dura:** Contains **The Bazaar**, a massive open-air marketplace filled with exotic and black-market goods.
* **Lower Dura:** Slums, tenements, and dangerous back-alleys. Includes **Callestan** (underworld turf) and **Malleon's Gate** (goblinoid enclave).

## Notable Points of Interest

* **The Bazaar:** An ever-changing maze of tents, shops, and cutpurses.
* **Clifftop Adventurer's Guild:** The primary hub for explorers looking for mercenary contracts or expeditions to Xen'drik.
* **[[gold-dragon-inn|Gold Dragon Inn (Dura Branch)]]:** A local branch operated by House Ghallanda.

## Annual Events

* **Race of Eight Winds:** A famous, highly competitive aerial race held every year, causing intense betting, rivalries, and brawls across Dura.
