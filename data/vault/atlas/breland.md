# Kingdom of Breland

Breland is the most populous, industrial, and democratic of the Five Nations of Khorvaire. Blending rich agricultural heartlands along the Dagger River with soaring industrial hubs like [[sharn]], Breland combines old-world monarchy with progressive civic ideals.

Its ruler, the aging King Boranel, is widely beloved, though rising democratic factions inside Parliament are actively campaigning to abolish the monarchy upon his death.

## Key Hubs & Features

* **[[sharn]]:** Known as the City of Towers, it is the economic and cultural center of the continent.
* **[[wroat]]:** The formal capital city, housing Parliament and the royal court of King Boranel.
* **The Southern Borders:** Heavily fortified border region bordering the deadly mist of the [[mournland]].

## Post-War Climate

Breland accepted more Cyran refugees following [[day-of-mourning|The Day of Mourning]] than any other nation, leading to social tension in working-class districts like [[dura|Dura]].
