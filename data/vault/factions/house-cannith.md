# House Cannith

House Cannith is the human Dragonmarked House that bears the **Mark of Making**. As the driving force behind Khorvaire’s magical industrial revolution, Cannith controls the guilds of tinkers, fabricators, architects, and alchemists. They are famously responsible for creating the warforged, the [[concept-lightning-rail|Lightning Rail]], and the processing methods for [[concept-dragonshards|Eberron and Siberys dragonshards]].

Since the Destruction of Cyre during [[lore-last-war|The Last War]], the house has been fractured into three competing branches, with its traditional ancestral seat completely destroyed.

## Internal Schism (The Three Barons)

Following the loss of the Gorgon's Forge in Cyre, House Cannith lacks a single unified leader:

* **Cannith South ([[sharn]]):** Led by Baron Merrix d'Cannith, focusing on secret warforged research, schema recovery, and industrial innovation.
* **Cannith West (Aundair):** Led by Baron Jorlanna d'Cannith, focusing on diplomacy, traditional crafting guilds, and rebuilding house prestige.
* **Cannith East (Karrnath):** Led by Baron Zorlan d'Cannith, focusing on martial crafting, siege weaponry, and alchemical warfare.

## Operations & Local Presence

In major urban centers like [[sharn]], House Cannith operates massive forge-holds, research enclaves, and public guildhalls. They maintain close economic ties with local authorities but frequently clash with unregulated magical syndicates.
