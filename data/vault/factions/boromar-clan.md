# The Boromar Clan

The Boromar Clan is the most powerful organized crime syndicate in [[sharn]]. Founded by halfling immigrants from the Talenta Plains, the clan has spent decades embedding itself into the political, economic, and social fabric of the city. To the public, many Boromar leaders appear as philanthropic halfling aristocrats and city councilors; behind closed doors, they run extortion rings, gambling dens, smuggling pipelines, and black-market dreamlily operations.

Despite their criminal nature, the Boromars maintain a status-quo policy, keeping street violence low to avoid disrupting legitimate commerce and council interests.

## Key Operations & Revenue

* **Smuggling & Contraband:** Controls the flow of illegal narcotics (especially dreamlily) shipped through [[dura|Dura's lower docks]].
* **Protection Rackets:** Extorts small businesses, taverns like the [[gold-dragon-inn|Gold Dragon Inn (Dura Branch)]], and open-air markets across Lower and Middle Sharn.
* **Political Corruption:** Bribes high-ranking officers within the [[sharn-watch|Sharn Watch]] and holds leveraged votes on the City Council.

## Current Conflicts

* **War with the Tarkanan Syndicate:** A bitter turf war has erupted against the [[sharn-tarkanan-syndicate|Tarkanan Syndicate]], an organization of assassins and thieves bearing aberrant dragonmarks who are actively cutting into Boromar protection money in [[dura|Dura]].
* **Dragonmarked Ties:** Maintains a lucrative, clandestine agreement with **House Jorasco** for smuggling healing supplies and specialized contraband across national borders.
